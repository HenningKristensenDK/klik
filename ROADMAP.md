# Roadmap

Klik grows one feature at a time, each as a new version. Ideas and votes: [issues](https://github.com/HenningKristensenDK/klik/issues).

## 1.0: first Microsoft Store version
- Klik 1.0.0 is 0.3.5 plus fixes. Later: 1.1 and 1.2 add features, 1.0.x fixes bugs.
- A long screenshot opens again from the gallery: larger than the screen, it opens as it is, in the tall view that scrolls, with the crop handles on the image. Cropping saves over the file, and the gallery picture updates.
- The gallery shows dates as 2026.11.25 - 12:04.
- A screenshot reopened from the gallery sits on your screen as it is, under the same light navy as after a capture, instead of on dark Ink, so you still see what lies behind.
- Around a framed area the screen is RTPM Navy at 50 % (was 55 %), after a capture and on a reopened screenshot.

## 0.3
- 0.3.5: the gallery shows dates as 2026.11.26 19:36.
- 0.3.4: Klik's own icon, the RTPM mark with a dot in the centre, navy on a transparent background, also at the start of the toolbar; right-click on the taskbar icon no longer repeats "Take screenshot", since a click on the icon already takes one; it lists "Gallery" instead. The gallery shows every screenshot; click one to mark or crop it again.
- 0.3.3: a watermark in the framed area shows that the mouse wheel makes a long image. Crop: drag the small handles on the frame, also on a long screenshot; `Ctrl+Z` undoes it. Studio is hidden for 1.0, so a click on the Klik icon takes a screenshot. "Send feedback" in the tray menu.
- 0.3.2: move a marking by dragging it; click it, then `Del` or its small x deletes it; a colour click recolours it; double-click a comment to edit it. The screen around a framed area is lighter (RTPM Navy 55 %).
- 0.3.1: the version number shows, small, in the editor's hint and next to "Klik Studio". Around a framed area the screen is RTPM Navy at 80 %, so the eye stays on the area; while you pick the area it is a light 30 %, so you see what to frame.
- 0.3.0 Long screenshot: select an area, then turn the mouse wheel. Klik scrolls the window below and stitches one tall image, with a fixed header kept once. `Esc` saves and copies it; a click opens it in the editor, where the wheel scrolls the image so you can mark all of it.

## 0.2
- Screenshot of the screen under the mouse, copied the moment you frame it.
- Highlighter, numbered arrow, arrow with comment, pen, line.
- Claude extension: Claude sees your screenshots and passes work between sessions.
- **Klik Studio:** turn screenshots into a 4:5 LinkedIn carousel or 16:9 slides, exported as PDF + PNG. Claude can draft the story.
- English, and Danish when Windows runs in Danish.
- 0.2.1: everything Klik saves is in `Pictures\Klik` (screenshots, `Inbox`, `Studio`), never in Documents. Files are named `Klik 2026.11.17 19.30 001.png`, numbered through the day.
- 0.2.2: a "Studio" button (key `S`) in the editor turns the screenshot you just took into a slide in Klik Studio. One click instead of three on a hidden tray icon.
- 0.2.3: in Studio's gallery, click a screenshot to add it as a slide, or click its pencil to add more markings in the editor. The slide updates by itself.
- 0.2.4: click the tray icon to open Klik Studio (screenshots start from the hotkey). New blue and white app icon.
- 0.2.7: Studio slide overview with small slide drawings, drag to reorder, delete with Undo, no duplicate slides from the gallery; highlight by clicking words. Profile edited by clicking it on the slide; large portrait cover.
- 0.2.8: Studio fixes from the Windows test: drag a slide works on Windows; the slide keeps its shape in a wide window; clicking your name on the slide opens the profile again.
- 0.2.6: Esc always closes the editor, also when it opened without the keyboard; the hotkey brings an open editor forward.
- 0.2.5: the RTPM app icon replaces the Klik icon. A click on the Klik icon on the taskbar opens Klik Studio; right-click it for "Take screenshot" or "Klik Studio". "New screenshot" in Studio takes one without the hotkey.

## Next
- **Studio 2:** rethought around one job, from your screenshots to a finished post or deck, with Claude writing the story.
- **0.4.0 Video of the area:** one video button (key `R`), webcam bubble, voice, 16:9, 4:5 or 1:1, MP4 to `Videos\Klik` and the clipboard.
- **Microsoft Store:** one-click install, signed, automatic updates.
- **Studio canvas and crop:** combine screenshots on one free canvas, and crop a screenshot so app text stays readable on a phone.
- **Step capture:** one screenshot per click, as a numbered guide.

## Later
- Cursor ring and click ripple in videos (0.4.1).
- LinkedIn posts from a recording: Claude plans the cuts and captions, Klik renders 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9 video.
- Claude writes the text for your step guides and posts.
- Long screenshot: Klik scrolls by itself after one click, and up-scroll and side-scroll.
